How to Play Music Triad

The card you play is the attacker!
  1. Choose a card, then place it in an empty square.
  2. Your new card attacks opponent cards directly above, below, left, or right.
  3. Notes battle notes. Rests battle rests.
  4. The side with the longer beat value captures the shorter one.
  5. Equal beat values tie, so nothing flips.
  6. Only the card just played attacks. Cards already on the board do not attack back.

Legendary Cards

Fermata: always wins on its side. It automatically captures when attacking and cannot be captured from that side when defending.
Conductor: when played, automatically flips every adjacent opponent card.

Winning

When all 9 spaces are filled, whoever controls more cards wins.
